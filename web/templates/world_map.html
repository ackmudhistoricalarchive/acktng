Oasis-Pyramid Corridor

The civilizational spine running south from the Eastern Desert through the three oases and three pyramids to Scorching Sands, Saltglass Reach, and the maritime port of Mafdet.

Five-City Network

The co-dependent chain linking Kiess, Midgaard, Kowloon, Rakuen, and Mafdet. The chain only functions when all five are present.

Desert-to-Sea Route
