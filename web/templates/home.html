The World of ACK!TNG

ACK!TNG is the living edge of the ACK! lineage — a text world under active development, with new areas, evolving lore, and a playerbase that shapes its ongoing story. Descended from Diku → Merc → ACK!, it carries decades of accumulated design wisdom while continuing to grow in new directions.

The lore collected here reflects the world as it stands today: its nations, its gods, its scars, and its unresolved tensions. Stories, area descriptions, and in-game lore entries all feed into a unified picture of a world that takes itself seriously.
